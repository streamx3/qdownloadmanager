Download Helper settings

Your websites

Download buttons appear on images on these domains and their subdomains. Avatars and tiny images are skipped.

Use this for image CDNs on a different domain. Chrome will ask for access when you add a new domain.

Transfer method

Browser transfer sends the source page’s referrer with the image request and passes the file bytes to the app, preserving animated GIFs. It makes a new request; it does not extract the page’s already-loaded image. URL mode does not use your browser session and may receive a watermarked image.

Connect the desktop app

  1. Open Download Helper and choose a download folder.
  2. Click Register Chrome bridge in the desktop app.
  3. Open this extension’s popup and click Connect.
